Markets & Trading Track

9 tutorials

From FX spot to risk engines: the full markets education path, in the order a new joiner would learn it on a trading floor.

  1. FX Essentials Beginner
    Spot, forwards, swaps and NDFs — the four instruments behind almost every FX support ticket.
  2. Fixed Income Essentials Intermediate
    Bonds, the price-yield seesaw, and yield curves — the math behind the biggest asset class.
  3. Futures & Options Intermediate
    Obligation versus right — the single distinction that explains both instruments.
  4. Trade Lifecycle Walkthrough Beginner
    From order entry to reconciliation — every stage of a trade's life, and what production support does at each.

  6. Valuation Methods Intermediate
    DCF, multiples, bond and option valuation — how the market decides what something is worth.
  7. Risk Measures: VaR & Stress Advanced
    VaR, Stressed VaR, stress tests and Expected Shortfall — the numbers risk engines produce every night.
  8. Risk Measures (Greeks) Intermediate
    Delta, Gamma, Vega and VaR — the numbers risk engines compute all day, with worked examples.
  9. Multi-Asset Instruments Intermediate
    One desk trades them all: equities, bonds, FX, commodities and derivatives.
