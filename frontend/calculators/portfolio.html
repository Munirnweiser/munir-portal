Portfolio Risk & Return

Expected return and volatility of a two-asset portfolio. Diversification shows up in the correlation term: the lower the correlation, the lower the portfolio volatility.

Result

Formulas

E(Rp) = w1E(R1) + w2E(R2)

σp2 = w12σ12 + w22σ22 + 2w1w2ρσ1σ2

Expected return is a simple weighted average, but variance is not: the cross-term 2w1w2ρσ1σ2 is where diversification lives. At ρ = 1 there is no diversification benefit; at ρ = −1 risk can be eliminated entirely at the right weights.
