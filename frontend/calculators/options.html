Option Payoffs at Expiry

Profit-and-loss diagrams for the four basic single-option positions. Pick a position, set the strike and premium, and inspect the payoff across a spot range.

Result

Formulas

Long call: max(S−K, 0) − premium   ·   Long put: max(K−S, 0) − premium

Short call: premium − max(S−K, 0)   ·   Short put: premium − max(K−S, 0)

Breakeven is K + premium for calls and K − premium for puts, long or short. Long positions have limited loss (the premium) and the call has unlimited upside; short positions mirror this — limited gain, and the short call has unlimited downside. These are expiry payoffs; before expiry, time value changes the picture.
