Bond Price & Yield to Maturity

Price a fixed-coupon bond by discounting every cash flow at the yield, or back out the yield-to-maturity implied by a market price (bisection).

Result

Formula

P = Σ C/(1+y)t + F/(1+y)n   for t = 1 … n

where C is the periodic coupon, F the face value, y the periodic yield and n the number of periods. YTM is the value of y that makes this price equal the observed market price.

Clean price is shown (accrued interest excluded). A bond priced below par trades at a discount; above par, at a premium.
