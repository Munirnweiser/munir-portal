Sharpe Ratio

Risk-adjusted return: how much excess return a portfolio earns per unit of total volatility.

Result

Formula

Sharpe = [E(Rp) − Rf] / σp

The numerator is the excess return over the risk-free rate; the denominator is total volatility. A higher Sharpe ratio means more return per unit of risk. As a rough guide, above 1 is good, above 2 very good — but the ratio assumes returns are roughly symmetric and works best comparing similar strategies over the same period.
