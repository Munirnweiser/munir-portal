NPV & IRR

Net present value of a cash-flow series at your discount rate, and the internal rate of return — the discount rate that makes NPV equal zero, found by bisection.

Result

Formulas

NPV = Σ CFt / (1+r)t   for t = 0 … n

IRR is the rate r* such that NPV(r*) = 0. It is found here by bisection on the interval (−99.99%, +1000%+), expanding upward if no sign change is detected.

A project can have multiple IRRs (or none) when cash flows change sign more than once. NPV remains the decision rule: accept when NPV > 0.
