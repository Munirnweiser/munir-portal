FX Forward Rate

The outright forward rate from covered interest rate parity: borrow in one currency, convert spot, invest in the other, and lock the reverse conversion.

Result

Formula

F = S · (1 + rquote·T) / (1 + rbase·T)

Discrete compounding with a 360-day money-market year (T = days / 360). The continuous-time equivalent is F = S·e(rquote−rbase)T. When the quote-currency rate exceeds the base-currency rate, the forward trades at a premium to spot; otherwise at a discount. Forward points are quoted as (F − S) × 10,000.
