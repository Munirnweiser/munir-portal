Duration & Convexity

Interest-rate risk measures for a fixed-coupon bond: Macaulay duration (weighted average time to cash flows), modified duration (first-order price sensitivity) and convexity (second-order curvature).

Result

Formulas

Macaulay duration = Σ t·wt   ·   Modified duration = Macaulay / (1+y)   ·   Convexity = Σ t(t+1)·wt / (1+y)2

where wt = PV(CFt) / price is the present-value weight of each cash flow and y is the periodic yield. Durations are annualised by dividing by the payment frequency; convexity by the square of the frequency.

Interpretation: a modified duration of D means the bond price moves roughly −D × Δy for a small yield change Δy. DV01 is the cash change for a 1 bp move. Convexity refines the estimate for larger moves: ΔP/P ≈ −DmodΔy + ½·Convexity·(Δy)2.
