WACC — Weighted Average Cost of Capital

The blended cost of a firm's financing: equity and debt weighted by market value, with debt adjusted for the tax shield on interest.

Result

Formula

WACC = (E/V)·Re + (D/V)·Rd·(1 − Tc),  where V = E + D

Weights use market values, not book values. Debt is cheaper than equity and its after-tax cost Rd(1−Tc) reflects the tax deductibility of interest — the reason leverage lowers WACC up to the point where distress costs dominate.
