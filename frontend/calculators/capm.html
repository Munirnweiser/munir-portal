CAPM — Expected Return

The Capital Asset Pricing Model: an asset's expected return equals the risk-free rate plus its beta times the market risk premium.

Result

Formula

E(Ri) = Rf + βi · [E(Rm) − Rf]

The bracketed term is the market risk premium — the extra return investors demand for holding the market instead of the risk-free asset. Beta measures how much of that premium the asset earns: β = 1 moves with the market, β > 1 amplifies it, β < 1 dampens it.
