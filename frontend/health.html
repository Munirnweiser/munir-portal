BMR & TDEE

  • BMR: kcal/day
  • TDEE: kcal/day

How to read this

  • Under 18.5 — underweight
  • 18.5 – 24.9 — healthy range
  • 25 – 29.9 — overweight
  • 30+ — obese (class I from 30, II from 35, III from 40)

BMR uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation: men = 10·kg + 6.25·cm − 5·age + 5; women = 10·kg + 6.25·cm − 5·age − 161. TDEE multiplies BMR by the activity factor.

Disclaimer: this page is for education only and is not medical advice. BMI is a screening tool — it does not measure body fat and can misclassify muscular people. Talk to a clinician about health decisions.
