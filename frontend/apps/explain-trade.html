Explain My Trade

Paste a FIX 35=8 ExecutionReport and get a plain-English walkthrough of exactly what happened to your order.

Paste an ExecutionReport

Delimiters |, ^A or real SOH bytes all work.

How to read an ExecutionReport

  • 150 (ExecType) tells you what just happened — this single report's event.
  • 39 (OrdStatus) tells you the current state of the order after this report.
  • 14 / 6 / 151 are the running totals: filled so far, average price, still working.
  • 32 / 31 are this fill only — one report can carry one fill leg.
  • 10 (CheckSum) must equal the byte-sum mod 256, or the message is corrupt.
