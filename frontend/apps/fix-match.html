FIX Tag Match

Sixteen cards, eight pairs. Match every FIX tag number with its field name — the tags you will see on every trading floor.

How it works

  • 8 pairs — each pair is a FIX tag number and its field name (35 ↔ MsgType, 49 ↔ SenderCompID, …).
  • Flip two cards. A tag matches its name, not another number.
  • The clock starts on your first flip. Fewer moves, better rank.
